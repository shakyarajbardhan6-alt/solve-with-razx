Isolate the term with x
Subtract 5 from both sides.

Solve for x

x = 12 ÷ 2 → x = 6

Is Doubt Solver free to use?+

There is no charge to use this demonstration. Availability may depend on the service hosting the site and its AI provider limits.

Can I ask questions in Hindi?+

Yes. Ask in Hindi or Hinglish and the tutor is instructed to reply in the same style.

Are AI answers always correct?+

No. AI can make mistakes or miss context. Check important answers against your course materials or ask a teacher.

Where are my previous questions saved?+

Your history is stored in local storage in this browser. It is not a synced account, and clearing browser data may remove it.
