THE DOUBT SOLVER IDEA
01 / THE TOOL

What is Doubt Solver?

Doubt Solver is a public educational platform where students can ask academic questions and receive AI-generated explanations. It is designed to help break a problem into manageable steps across subjects such as mathematics, science, programming and English.

It is a learning aid, not a teacher, textbook, or answer key. Use it to explore ideas, check your reasoning and decide what to study next.

02 / THE PROCESS

How it works

Choose a subject and write your question in your own words. The server sends the question to an AI provider to generate a student-friendly response. That response is shown in your browser, where you can copy it or revisit it later using the local history.

01 You ask→02 AI explains→03 You explore
03 / THE PURPOSE

Learning comes first

Explanations aim to show the reasoning behind an answer, especially for numerical questions. The intention is to encourage understanding and follow-up questions, rather than replace a student's own work, classroom discussion or practice.

04 / A NECESSARY LIMIT

AI can be wrong

AI-generated answers may be inaccurate, incomplete, out of date, or confidently phrased despite being incorrect. They may misunderstand a prompt or miss the context of your course. Always verify important information with trusted learning materials or a qualified educator, and don't rely on the service for high-stakes decisions.

Do not include sensitive personal information in questions. Learn more in the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
